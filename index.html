Privacy Policy

Luviio ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and company name when you fill out contact forms or request consultations.
  • Project Details: Information about your business requirements, project scope, and preferences shared during discussions.
  • Account Data: If you create an account on our platform, we store your login credentials (encrypted), profile settings, and usage history.
  • Payment Information: Billing details processed securely through third-party payment providers (Stripe, FPX). We do not store complete credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Communications: Records of emails, chat messages, and support tickets you exchange with our team.

We also automatically collect certain information when you visit our website:

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, click patterns, and navigation paths.
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device type, screen resolution, and IP address (anonymized where possible).
  • Cookies: Small data files used to enhance user experience, remember preferences, and analyze traffic patterns.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our services and deliver the products or services you request.
  • To respond to your inquiries, provide customer support, and send technical notices and updates.
  • To communicate with you about new services, features, promotions, or changes to our offerings (with your consent).
  • To improve our website, develop new services, and personalize your experience.
  • To protect against fraud, unauthorized transactions, and ensure the security of our systems.
  • To comply with legal obligations, enforce our terms, and resolve disputes.

3. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your data only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party vendors who assist us in operating our platform (hosting providers, analytics services, email delivery). These parties are bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with any merger, acquisition, sale of assets, or transfer of all or a portion of our business.
  • Your Consent: With your explicit permission for specific purposes.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission.
  • Encrypted storage of sensitive data at rest.
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments.
  • Access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel only.
  • Secure backup and recovery procedures.

While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences and settings.
  • Analyze website traffic and usage patterns using tools like Google Analytics.
  • Provide personalized content and advertisements (where applicable).

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain functionality of our website.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request copies of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to processing of your data for direct marketing or legitimate interests.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Withdraw previously given consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@luviio.com.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law. When data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on this page and updating the "Last revised" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Refund Policy

At Luviio, we strive to deliver exceptional value through our digital services. This Refund Policy outlines the circumstances under which refunds may be issued for our services and subscription plans.

1. General Principle

Due to the custom nature of our work — including website design, development, consulting, and ongoing support services — we generally do not offer refunds once work has commenced or been delivered. This is because significant time, resources, and intellectual effort are invested from the moment a project begins.

2. Subscription Plans (Monthly Recurring)

  • Cancellation: You may cancel your subscription plan at any time. Upon cancellation, you retain access to paid services until the end of the current billing period. No prorated refunds are provided for partial months.
  • No Refunds for Used Services: Since services are delivered continuously throughout the billing period, refunds are not issued for portions of a billing cycle during which services were active.
  • Billing Errors: If you believe you were charged incorrectly (duplicate charge, wrong amount, unauthorized charge), please contact us within 30 days of the charge date. We will investigate and issue a refund if an error is confirmed.

3. One-Time Projects & Custom Work

  • Before Work Begins: If you cancel before we commence any work on your project, you are entitled to a full refund of any payments made, minus any non-refundable deposit explicitly stated in your proposal or agreement.
  • After Work Commences: Once work has begun, payments become non-refundable as they represent compensation for time, resources, and progress made. This applies regardless of whether the project is completed or cancelled mid-way.
  • Scope Changes: If you choose to reduce the scope of a project after work has started, the original fee remains payable for work already performed or committed. Adjustments may be made for clearly unstarted future phases, at our discretion.

4. Exceptional Circumstances

We may consider refund requests on a case-by-case basis in exceptional situations, such as:

  • Service Failure: If we fail to deliver agreed-upon services as specified in our contract or statement of work, despite being given a reasonable opportunity to remedy the deficiency, a proportional refund may be issued for undelivered or defective work.
  • Duplicate Payments: Accidental duplicate payments will be fully refunded upon verification.
  • System Error: Technical errors resulting in incorrect charges will be rectified with appropriate refunds or credits.

Note: Dissatisfaction with creative direction or subjective preferences does not qualify for a refund, provided that the delivered work meets the agreed specifications and professional standards.

5. How to Request a Refund

To submit a refund request, please:

  1. Email us at billing@luviio.com with the subject line "Refund Request – [Your Company Name]"

  3. Allow 5–10 business days for us to review your request and respond.

If approved, refunds will be processed within 14 business days to the original payment method. Please note that depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take additional time for the refunded amount to appear in your account.

6. Chargebacks & Disputes

Before initiating a chargeback or payment dispute with your bank or credit card provider, we ask that you contact us first to resolve the issue amicably. Unwarranted chargebacks may result in immediate suspension of services and additional administrative fees.
