Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the one-time payment?

All plans include complete design, development, testing, deployment, and the specified support period. This covers everything from initial consultation to final handover with documentation. Hosting and domain renewals are billed separately based on usage.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes! We offer flexible payment plans for Professional and Enterprise packages. You can split payments into 2-3 installments over the project duration. Contact us to discuss options that fit your budget.

What happens after the support period ends?

After your included support period ends, you can extend coverage through our maintenance packages starting from MYR 299/month, or opt for pay-as-you-go for specific updates and changes. We also offer annual retainer agreements for ongoing support.

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on complexity. A typical Starter project takes 2-4 weeks, Professional projects 4-8 weeks, and Enterprise projects are scoped individually. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our discovery phase.

Can I request changes during development?

Absolutely! We use an iterative approach with regular check-ins and review milestones. You'll have opportunities to provide feedback at each stage. Minor adjustments are included within scope, and we'll discuss any major changes as they arise.
