Luviio Team Overview

Luviio is a growing technology brand based in Kedah, Malaysia, founded in 2025. Built with a vision to create simple, useful, and accessible digital tools, Luviio is currently expanding from local roots toward an international audience.

Vision

Start local, build global. Deliver simple and impactful tools to users worldwide while maintaining quality and simplicity.

Approach

Remote-first, no physical office. Lean operations focused on what matters — building better tools, faster.

Principle

Build useful products that solve real problems. Don't chase attention — earn trust through consistent delivery.
