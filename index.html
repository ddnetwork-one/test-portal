Danial Danish — Founder & CEO

Danial Danish - Founder & CEO at Luviio

Danial Danish's journey didn't begin with Luviio.

It started in Sungai Petani, with something much smaller — a project called DDNetwork. There was no team, no office, and no roadmap. Just a laptop, a growing skillset, and the decision to start anyway.

The early days were quiet.

Finding customers wasn't easy. There were no ads running, no big announcements — just effort and patience. Growth came slowly, mostly through word of mouth. One client turned into another. One good experience became a recommendation.

It wasn't fast, but it was real.

That period shaped Danial Danish's mindset: don't chase attention — earn trust.

Over time, DDNetwork became known within its circle. Not because it was everywhere, but because it consistently delivered. And that consistency built something more valuable than hype — credibility.

But staying small was never the long-term goal.

After years of building and understanding what users actually need, Danial Danish saw a bigger opportunity: to create something structured, scalable, and product-focused.

That idea became Luviio.

Building From Kedah

Based in Kedah, Luviio runs without a physical office — not as a limitation, but as a choice. Staying lean allows the team to focus on what actually matters: building better tools, faster.

From 2025 to the present, Danial Danish has been focused on growing Luviio into more than just a service — a platform for simple, useful, and accessible tools.

The vision is clear: Start local, build global.

Luviio may be built from Kedah, but the ambition goes far beyond it. Step by step, the goal is to reach users worldwide — without losing the simplicity and quality that started everything.

A Message from Danial Danish

You don't need perfect conditions to start.

You don't need a full team, a big office, or everything figured out.

Starting from Kedah with limited resources didn't stop the journey — and it won't stop yours either.

Growth takes time.
Recognition takes time.
Success takes time.

But if you focus on real value, people will notice.

Start small. Stay consistent. Build something that matters.

Nurul Nabila — Co-founder & Project Lead

Nurul Nabila - Co-founder & Project Lead at Luviio

Every vision needs structure. Every idea needs execution.

That's where Nurul Nabila comes in.

As Co-founder and Project Lead, Bella plays a critical role in turning ideas into real, finished products. While many see the final result, her work happens behind the scenes — in planning, organizing, and making sure every moving part works together.

The Strength of Clarity

Bella's strength lies in clarity.

In fast-moving environments, things can easily become chaotic — missed timelines, unclear expectations, and misaligned teams. Bella ensures that doesn't happen.

She focuses on:

  • Clear direction
  • Realistic timelines
  • Consistent delivery

But her role goes beyond project management.

Bella understands that great products don't come from pressure alone — they come from teams that are motivated, aligned, and supported. She keeps the balance between pushing for excellence and maintaining a healthy workflow.

Every project at Luviio passes through that balance.

Solving Problems Properly

Challenges are part of the process — tight deadlines, changing requirements, unexpected problems. But Bella approaches each one with the same mindset:

Solve it properly, not quickly.

That consistency is what allows Luviio to deliver work that doesn't just meet expectations — it often exceeds them.

A Message from Nurul Nabila

Execution is what separates ideas from results.

Many people have good ideas. Few follow through.

If you're building something:

• Plan properly
• Stay organized
• Don't rush the process

Success isn't about doing everything at once. It's about doing things right, step by step.

Putri Erinna — Head of Client Relations

Putri Erinna - Head of Client Relations at Luviio

Behind every successful product is trust.

Putri Erinna is responsible for building and maintaining that trust at Luviio.

As Head of Client Relations, she connects two worlds — the client's vision and the technical execution. Her role ensures that nothing gets lost in translation.

The Communication Bridge

Because often, the biggest problems don't come from code — they come from miscommunication.

Putri focuses on clarity.

She listens carefully to what clients need, breaks it down into actionable direction, and ensures the team understands it fully. At the same time, she keeps clients informed, updated, and confident throughout the process.

Her work is built on three things:

  • Consistency
  • Relationships

Partners, Not Transactions

For Putri, it's not just about completing a project — it's about building long-term connections. Clients aren't treated as transactions, but as partners.

That approach creates something more valuable than a one-time success:

Trust that lasts.

In a fast-paced digital environment, that human connection is what sets Luviio apart.
